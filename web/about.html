About Base64 Crafter

Base64 Crafter is a simple and efficient online tool designed for developers and users to easily convert files and text to Base64, and decode Base64 back to original files or content.

  • Convert files to Base64 strings for easy sharing and embedding.
  • Decode Base64 strings back to files in a secure and accurate way.
  • Support for common file types like images, documents, and text.
  • Minimalistic UI for fast, distraction-free conversion.
  • No data is stored — everything runs in your browser.
