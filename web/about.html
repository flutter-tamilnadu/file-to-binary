About Hex Crafter

Hex Crafter is a fast and user-friendly online utility built for developers and tech enthusiasts to convert files and text into hexadecimal format, and decode hex back to original files or content.

  • Convert files to hexadecimal strings for inspection, debugging, or transmission.
  • Decode hex strings back to files with full accuracy and security.
  • Supports common file types including images, executables, text, and more.
  • Simple, minimalistic interface for a smooth conversion experience.
  • 100% browser-based — your data never leaves your device.
